# DGG SFAP — Student Guide

Welcome to the Délı̨nę Got'ı̨nę Government Student Financial Assistance Program (SFAP) portal.

---

## Getting Started

1. Go to the portal URL (provided by DGG Education)
2. Click **Sign Up** and fill in your details
3. Complete your profile — this pre-fills your application forms
4. Choose the right form from the dashboard

---

## Which Form to Use

| Situation | Form |
|-----------|------|
| First-time application | **Form A** — New Student Application |
| Renewing each semester | **Form C** — Continuing Student Renewal |
| Something changed mid-semester | **Form D** — Change of Information |
| Practicum/work term | **Form F** — Practicum Evaluation |
| Graduation bursary | **Form G** — Graduation Award |
| Appealing a decision | **Form H** — Appeal |
| Academic scholarship | Scholarship form |
| Emergency hardship | Hardship Bursary |

---

## Form A — New Student Application

For students applying for the first time.

**What you need:**
- Transcripts (high school or post-secondary)
- Letter of Intent
- Status Card (Beneficiary ID)
- Void cheque (for direct deposit)
- Reference letter (optional but helpful)
- Registrar's email address (DGG sends enrollment verification to them directly)

**Steps:**
1. Confirm your personal information
2. Enter your institution and program details
3. Enter your direct deposit banking information
4. Upload documents and sign the declaration
5. Submit — you will see a confirmation with a reference number

**After submission:**  
DGG automatically emails an enrollment verification form to your registrar. They have 14 days to complete and return it.

---

## Form C — Continuing Student Renewal

Submit this every semester after your first. Your information from Form A pre-fills automatically.

**What you need:**
- Latest transcripts
- Enrollment confirmation for the new semester

**Steps:**
1. Review your information (pre-filled from previous application)
2. Upload transcripts and enrollment confirmation
3. Sign and submit

---

## Form D — Change of Information

Submit this if anything changes mid-semester that could affect your funding:
- Dropping or withdrawing from a course
- Changing your institution or program
- Change in number of dependents
- Change in contact information
- Change in SFA (Social Financial Assistance) status

Submit as soon as the change occurs. Delayed reporting may require repayment.

---

## Drafts & Autosave

Your form progress is automatically saved to your browser as you type. If you close the tab, your draft is still there next time you open the form. The draft is cleared when you successfully submit.

---

## Checking Your Application Status

Dashboard → My Applications.  
Possible statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Pending | Under review by SSW staff |
| Forwarded | Sent to Director for approval |
| Reviewed | Staff has reviewed; awaiting next step |
| Accepted | Approved — funding will be processed |
| Rejected | Not approved — see decision notes |
| More Info Required | Staff needs additional documents from you |

---

## Payments

Once approved, payments are released monthly by Finance. Funds are deposited directly to the bank account you provided.

If your application was approved after the semester start date (late application), you will receive back-pay for the missed months.

---

## Appeals

If your application was denied and you disagree, submit **Form H** with:
- Reason for your appeal
- Any supporting evidence
- Policy reference (if applicable)

Appeals are reviewed by the Director first, then escalated to DGGR Official, then CEO if needed.

---

## Privacy & Security

- Your SIN and banking details are encrypted and never shared with third parties
- Your identity is verified using a privacy-protected hash (not reversible)
- All documents are stored securely in Canadian servers
- You can request deletion of your data by contacting DGG Education
